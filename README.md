# CV Physiology Exam Practice

Unofficial, student-made practice exam for the cardiology block of MEDC 30504 (Cell & Organ Physiology).

- 113 original multiple-choice questions: cardiac cycle & PV loops, cardiac output & Guyton curves, hemodynamics & microcirculation, membrane potentials & muscle, electrophysiology & 12-lead ECGs, autonomic control & integration
- Computer-generated 12-lead ECGs and rhythm strips (click to enlarge, built-in calipers)
- Full explanations for every option, shown right after you answer
- Strengths/weaknesses report with charts; progress is saved in your browser only

Not affiliated with or reviewed by course faculty. AI-assisted; verify against lecture materials.

Static site, no build step: `index.html` plus `ecg.js` (ECG renderer) and `q-*.js` (question bank).
